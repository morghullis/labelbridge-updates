LabelBridge

Prints your Shopify order labels by itself on your Windows PC or your Mac: USB, network or Wi‑Fi printers.

⬇  Download for WindowsWindows 10/11 64-bit · 52.3 MB
⬇  Download for MacApple Silicon (M1 or newer) · macOS 15+ · 64.8 MB
Version 1.19.0 · Published on October 7, 2026
1

Download and install

Windows: open the downloaded file. If you see “Windows protected your PC”, press More info → Run anyway.

Mac: open the .dmg and drag LabelBridge to Applications. If it won't open the first time: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.

2

Follow the guide

The app opens on the Step-by-step guide: printer, test, Shopify store and test order.

3

Done

Every paid order prints by itself. The app lives next to the clock (on a Mac, in the menu bar) and opens by itself when the computer starts.

What's new in version 1.19.0

  • Always on (Settings → General, on Windows): so LabelBridge prints even when nobody is using the PC. Each piece is turned on with a button and confirmed first:
    • Don't sleep when plugged in: when plugged in, the PC won't sleep or hibernate and closing the lid won't turn it off (the screen still turns off).
    • Wake up if put to sleep: if someone puts it to sleep, it wakes up by itself every 10 minutes and keeps printing.
    • Print without anyone signed in: when the PC starts, or when signed out, LabelBridge works in the background with your account (Windows asks once for your password, in its own security window; Windows keeps it, not LabelBridge). When you sign in, the window takes over without printing anything twice.
  • With the screen locked it already printed; with the PC turned off nothing can print.

All versions

Is a new version giving you trouble? Download an earlier one and install it on top: it keeps your store, printers and history. The app will tell you about new versions and you can skip them.
